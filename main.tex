\begin{abstract}
We introduce Gravit Truth Consensus, a decentralized epistemic aggregation system based on KL-geometry, mirror descent dynamics, and gossip averaging. We prove exponential convergence to a unique fixed point, establish Byzantine robustness with explicit threshold bounds, and demonstrate incentive compatibility via strictly proper scoring rules. Empirical results confirm theoretical contraction rates and reveal a sharp phase transition in adversarial regimes.
\end{abstract}
